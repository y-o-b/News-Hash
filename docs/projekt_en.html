News-Hash

A news archive for people who want to know what happened and what they can still rely on later.

News shapes how we see the world. It helps us make decisions, have conversations, and understand connections. News-Hash gives this information a stable place and preserves it so that its history remains traceable.

Why this project exists

The news world moves quickly. Reports appear, change, get expanded, or disappear again. What was easy to find yesterday can be difficult to locate tomorrow.

News-Hash offers a calm, reliable archive in response to this volatility. It collects news from different sources and shows which information was available at a particular point in time.

For curious people

News-Hash is for anyone who wants to look more closely: people interested in politics and society, observers of current events, and anyone who wants to form their own opinion.

The project encourages following developments over longer periods instead of chasing only the latest outrage.

What makes News-Hash special

It is not the loudest report that should matter, but the opportunity to form your own view later.

News with a history

Every archived report belongs to a timeline. This makes it easier to understand changes, new developments, and recurring topics.

Sources in view

Different news sources become visible together. This provides orientation and helps reveal different perspectives.

Traceable, not fleeting

News-Hash stands for careful handling of what was published. The archive is meant to show what was there, not rewrite a story afterwards.

Open to discovery

The project grows with the news and with its users' questions. Every source can offer a new way of looking at the world.

An archive that invites reflection

News-Hash does not aim to provide ready-made opinions. It wants to create a basis on which people can compare, contextualize, and ask questions for themselves. Good orientation emerges where information does not have to move on immediately.

Those who look at news with some distance often see more: What has changed? Which topics keep returning? Which perspectives might be missing? And which questions remain open?

Stay curious

News-Hash is an invitation to look more closely, discover connections, and give the flow of news a little more permanence.
